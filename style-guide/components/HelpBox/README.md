The Help box is a white `help` card with an 8px green rule down its left side, a short heading and one or two sentences about the choices on the form. It is always visible, directly under the Filter or "Choose your groups" heading and above the first step.

The consumer provides the sentence. Explain what each step picks, in plain words, in no more than three sentences.

- Do keep it above the form, so it is read before the choices.
- Do not turn it into a button that shows and hides the text: the site has no JavaScript.
- Do not put anything essential only in the Help box. Labels must stand on their own.
