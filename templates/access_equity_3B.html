What this shows

In this sample, people who delayed both services were more likely to be in the lowest income group and less likely to have private health insurance than people who delayed only the first.

And how we worked this out

We took everyone who delayed Service 1 because of cost. We split them into those who also delayed Service 2 and those who did not. Then we compared the two groups on income, private health insurance and long-term conditions.
